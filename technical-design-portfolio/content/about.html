About Me

I've been making games since before I could read; I remember leafing through old D&D books and copying the pictures to make my own board games. Besides game development, I love working on cars, thrifting, skateboarding, and spending time with my partner, Ray!

Inspirations

I can block out the eras of my life by my inspirations. I played Halo 3 when I was eight, and I remember being fascinated by the "behind the scenes" content included with the game. I think I decided right there that I wanted to be a game designer when I grew up. Before I left for college in 2019, I played Outer Wilds, and it completely blew my mind. It opened me up to welcoming new experiences and explorations. Recently, I played Skate Story by Sam Eng, and it taught me to really, really work at something, even when it seems impossible or absurd.
