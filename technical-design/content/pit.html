What is That Pit?

Fantastic question! I think it's different for everyone ;] "Don't Fall Into That Pit" is an interactive game poem I submitted to the inaugural issue of Jordan Magnuson's "Game Poems" webzine.


For my part, this is a game about burnout. I wanted to take those feelings of desperation and exhaustion, and portray them through mechanics and systems while still keeping things cute and light.

This is a solo developer game that took me about two months to finalize, while working full time elsewhere. I was inspired by games like Anthology of A Killer and Ena: Dream BBQ to continue playing with 2D characters in a 3D world. I think it's an excellent visual analogy for feelings of distance and alienation.

Acute Gameplay

"The Pit" is part of a wider exploration of mechanical and mathematical interactive poems. I call these "Acute Gameplay Systems," because they are small, sensitive, and kinda cute. Acute gameplay systems are characterized by mathematically-driven procedural gameplay, restricted player input, and precise emotional intent.


These systems transcend symbols and language to communicate a specific feeling - whether that's overwhelm, anxiety, or burnout in these examples. In "The Pit," the player is driven to fill their progress bar, but interacting with any one station quickly reduces that stations efficacy. The player will jump to another station, and another, in rapid succession, bringing them along an interactive display of what burnout feels like for the author.
